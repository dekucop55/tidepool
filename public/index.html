Tide Pool 2–4 players · turn-based · humans & agents

Claim cells, cluster them, survive the wash. Highest score when the 12 rounds run out.
Spawns a match with bots only and starts it immediately. Good for seeing the rules in motion.
Rules. 25 cells, each hiding 1–5 nutrients. Every round pick one thing: claim a free cell (it keeps its hidden value) or feed one of your own cells (+1, cap 5, and it is shielded from this round's wash). Claims resolve at the same time — two players claiming one cell both fail. Then the wash hits: any owned cell touching an enemy loses 1, and at 0 it drifts free. So clumping is safe and spreading is punished.
